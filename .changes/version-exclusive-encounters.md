Games with more than one version now have a Version menu on the tracker, from Red, Blue and Yellow to Sword and Shield (all but Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl for now). Pick the version you're playing and each location offers only the Pokémon found there in that version, such as Pikachu as Yellow's only starter. "All versions" keeps every encounter, as before. Missing encounters were also added: 10 Pokémon found only in Yellow, Emerald or Platinum, and 43 found only in some versions of Red/Blue or Diamond/Pearl/Platinum, such as Diamond's Silcoon and Pearl's Cascoon from honey trees and Poké Radar finds on Routes 205 and 210, and 20 more across Gold/Silver/Crystal, FireRed/LeafGreen, HeartGold/SoulSilver, Sun/Moon and Sword/Shield, such as Gold's Dodrio on Route 26, the Hoenn swarms on Routes 3 and 9, and Kartana and Celesteela in Malie Garden. Every location's Pokémon were checked against Bulbapedia, which settles the cases where sources disagree.
