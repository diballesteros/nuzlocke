Games with more than one version now have a Version menu on the tracker, from Red, Blue and Yellow to Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl. Pick the version you're playing and each location offers only the Pokémon found there in that version, such as Pikachu as Yellow's only starter, with Bulbapedia as the source. Locations also gain the version-exclusive Pokémon they were missing, and Lake of Outrage's misspelled Hatterene can now be picked. The Pokémon selector also labels the Pokémon you can't just find in the wild at a location, such as gifts, trades, Game Corner prizes, headbutt trees, swarms and one-off special encounters. "All versions" keeps every encounter, as before, and custom games don't have the menu.
