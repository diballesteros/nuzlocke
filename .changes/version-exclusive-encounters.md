Games with more than one version now have a Version menu on the tracker, from Red, Blue and Yellow to Sword and Shield (all but Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl for now). Pick the version you're playing and each location offers only the Pokémon found there in that version, such as Pikachu as Yellow's only starter. "All versions" keeps every encounter, as before. Ten Pokémon found only in Yellow, Emerald or Platinum were also added to their locations.
