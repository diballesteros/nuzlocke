Red, Blue and Yellow, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, and Diamond, Pearl and Platinum now have a Version menu on the tracker. Pick the version you're playing and each location offers only the Pokémon found there in that version, such as Pikachu as Yellow's only starter. "All versions" keeps every encounter, as before. Ten Pokémon found only in Yellow, Emerald or Platinum were also added to their locations.
